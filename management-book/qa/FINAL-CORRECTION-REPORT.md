## 8. Remaining limitations

1. **No Word / LibreOffice in this environment.** The DOCX was verified by parsing its XML (metadata, TOC entries, bookmarks, sections, tables) but was never opened in a word processor. Consequences to check on a real machine: how the 264 TOC page numbers resolve, how the 14 landscape sections paginate, and that no table spills a page.
2. **No EPUB reading system and no EPUBCheck.** `scripts/qa.py` imports `epubcheck` + `jdk4py`, neither of which is installed, so that single check reports «اجرا نشد» (66 of 67 checks pass). Package structure, OPF, nav/toc parity and markup were verified by script, but not validated by a conformance tool nor read in Apple Books/Calibre/Thorium.
3. **No browser.** The HTML edition was checked structurally, not rendered.
4. **Proof pages are rendered by the book's own engine** (`scripts/build_proofs.py`), not by a real reading system; they are indicative, not a substitute for §1–3.
5. **MCQ rebalancing is a content change.** Every key was re‑derived from the same correct option (verified 150/150) and stems/objectives were untouched, but human review of the reworded distractors is still advisable before print.
6. **Two of the four original "mismatches"** in the answer‑preservation check are intentional rewording (Q102, Q134) and two are the hamza normalisation (Q25, Q135); they are listed in `qa/mcq-verification.json`.
7. Nothing in this report is a claim of perfection: 100 % flawlessness is not claimed and cannot be established without the tools listed above.
